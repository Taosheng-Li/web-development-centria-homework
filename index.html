Strang neighbor

I think my neighbor Angela is a very interesting person. We live in the same residential quarter, and first met while walking our dogs; mine a poodle and hers a Pomeranian. I'd say she's a retiree in her mid-60s or so. I always walk my dog after 10 pm, and often meet her in the elevator, so we both got to know each other. Angela is very kind and funny. She is very caring and treats her pet as if it was her baby. One time, while chatting with her, she told me about how she used to live in a house in the city center that didn't have an elevator. The area around her house was bustling with traffic. Because of this, every time she took her dog for a walk, she had to be super cautious. But after retiring, she moved to our neighborhood. Here, we have all these huge grassy areas that are perfect for dog walking, and elevators in every building, which makes it easy for her and her dog to go up and down. But here's the funny part. Whenever she takes her dog downstairs for a walk, she actually talks to her dog as if the dog understands her. It's like she's having a conversation with her dog. For example, when her little furry friend steps in some mud on the road, she patiently says, "Sweetie, that's a dirty spot, let's go this way instead." Every time I see her talking to her dog, it cracks me up. It's just too amusing!
